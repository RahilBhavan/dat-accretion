NORWALK, CT, November 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — (NYSE: BMNR) Bitmine Immersion Technologies, Inc. (“Bitmine” or the “Company”), a Bitcoin and Ethereum Network company with a focus on the accumulation of crypto for long term investment, today announced Bitmine crypto + total cash & marketable securities + “moonshots” holdings totaling $14.3 billion.

As of November 8, 2026 at 2:00pm ET, the Company’s crypto holdings are comprised of 6,070,0921 ETH at $2,234 per ETH (per Coinbase NASDAQ: COIN), 216 Bitcoin (BTC), $180 million stake in Beast Industries, $75 million stake in Eightco Holdings (NASDAQ: ORBS) (“moonshots”) and total cash & marketable securities of $475 million2. Bitmine’s ETH holdings are 4.97% of the ETH supply (of 122.2 million ETH).

“Vitalik Buterin, founder of Ethereum, declared at Devcon 8 in Mumbai, ‘I’ve finally made up my mind, ETH is a store of value, ETH is money.’ This declaration by the founder of Ethereum, in my view, is a seismic shift, clarifying how ETH will capture value in this coming supercycle of tokenization and AI,” stated Thomas “Tom” Lee, Chairman of Bitmine.

“In my opinion, ETH was not a ‘cash flow’ token model, like HYPE or LIT, but rather pristine and trusted collateral, and reflective of how Ethereum is the Layer 1 chosen by the vast majority of financial institutions moving assets onto the blockchain,” continued Lee.

Vitalik further clarified his views with a X.com post (link here) expanding on why he believes “ETH as store of value (SoV).” The 3 reasons he described:

1.ETH as fee-burning is not as strong “as it seemed;”
2.Optimizing for fee-burning is not necessarily compatible with decentralization and capture resistance; and
3.Optimizing for SoV aligns with ETH’s strengths of decentralization and CROPS.

“In other words, ETH as a Store of Value is most consistent with the future roadmap of ETH and also the role ETH will play in the coming super tokenization supercycle,” added Lee.

Earlier in 2026, Bitmine launched MAVAN (the Made in America VAlidator Network), the institutional-grade staking platform. While MAVAN was originally developed to support Bitmine’s own Ethereum treasury, MAVAN has expanded to serve institutional investors, custodians, and ecosystem partners seeking best-in-class staking infrastructure. A portion of Bitmine’s ETH is already staked on the MAVAN platform.

As of November 8, 2026, Bitmine total staked ETH stands at 5,227,037 ($11.7 billion at $2,234 per ETH). “Bitmine has staked more ETH than other entities in the world. At scale (when Bitmine’s ETH is fully staked by MAVAN and its staking partners), the projected ETH staking rewards would be $359 million on an annualized basis, using BMNR’s trailing 7-day staking yield of 2.65% as of November 8, 2026. This compares to annualized staking rewards of $309 million based upon 5.2 million staked ETH (86% of the 6.07 million ETH held by Bitmine),” continued Lee.

About Bitmine

Bitmine Immersion Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: BMNR), together with its subsidiaries (“Bitmine” or the “Company”), is a blockchain technology infrastructure company operating across institutional digital asset staking and validation services, bitcoin mining, and strategic digital asset management. As the world’s leading Ethereum Treasury company, it implements an innovative digital asset strategy for institutional investors and public market participants. The Company provides institutional-grade staking and validation infrastructure—through which it earns staking rewards and validation income—alongside bitcoin mining activities. Bitmine holds digital assets strategically, generating yield on those holdings to support liquidity and capital formation. Since 2025, the Company has expanded its blockchain infrastructure capabilities, including developing and deploying MAVAN, its institutional staking and validation platform. The Company’s activities further include investments in early-stage blockchain opportunities (“moonshot” investments) and ancillary mining, hosting, and consulting services.
