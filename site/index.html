Method

The metric is Strategy's own net coins per share definition, applied to all three firms, taken from the glossary in Strategy's 2026-08-24 FWP. Full note: methodology.

N = C + (R − D − F) / p n = N / S m = s·S / (p·N) = s / (p·n)

C
coins held (BTC or ETH; SharpLink includes LsETH and weETH as-if redeemed)
R
USD assets: USD Reserve plus USD Cash (Strategy); cash and marketable securities (BitMine); balance-sheet cash (SharpLink)
D
notional of out-of-the-money converts and other debt
F
notional of perpetual preferred
S
fully diluted shares: common, awards, in-the-money converts
p, s
coin close and common close
q
preferred close / $100 notional (STRC for Strategy, BMNP for BitMine; SharpLink has no preferred)

Each action moves x dollars. With k = x / (p·S), the first-order change in n is:

#actionΔnadds when
1issue commonk·(1 − 1/m)m > 1
2buy back commonk·(1/m − 1)m < 1
3issue preferredk·(1 − 1/q)q > 1
4retire preferredk·(1/q − 1)q < 1
5buy or sell coins0neutral on day one

The break-even line is m = q. Strategy's rotation (1 and 4: issue common, retire preferred) adds while m > q, so it stops when STRC trades above $100 × m. BitMine's rotation (3 and 2: issue preferred, buy back common) adds while m < q, so it stops when BMNP trades below its liquidation preference × m. SharpLink has no preferred, so no rotation line applies: issuing common adds while m > 1 and buying back common adds while m < 1.

The bars use exact values, not the first-order formulas: each week's observed change in n is split in order into coin price, in-the-money flips, each action at its own disclosed price, carry (preferred dividends and convert interest), and a residual. Each step recomputes n. The residual is what the filings leave unexplained.
